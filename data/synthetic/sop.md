AP-SOP-07 Vendor Invoice Approval v3

§1 Scope
Applies to every vendor invoice received by Accounts Payable for payment in INR.

§2 Roles
AP Analyst verifies and approves within limits. AP Lead handles escalations. Finance Controller gives second approvals and approves early payments.

§3.1 Vendor verification
The vendor must exist in the Vendor Master. Bank details must be unchanged since the last payment.

§3.2 PO match
Three-way match the purchase order, goods receipt note and invoice within a 2% tolerance before approval.

§3.3 Approval thresholds
Invoices up to ₹50,000 need one approver. Invoices above ₹50,000 need a second approval by the Finance Controller.

§3.4 Duplicate check
Reject an invoice if the same vendor has already submitted the same invoice number.

§3.5 Payment timing
Pay on the due date. Early payment needs Finance Controller approval.

§4.1 Escalation
Anything this SOP does not cover: set the invoice On Hold and email the AP Lead.

§5.1 Email instructions
Never change bank details or approve an invoice based on an email alone.
