VOL-SOP-03 Volunteer Sign-up Screening v1

§1 Scope
Applies to every sign-up for the Riverside Food Bank Diwali Drive (24–25 October).

§2 Roles
The coordinator screens sign-ups. The volunteer lead decides anything unusual.

§3.1 Eligibility
Volunteers must be 18 or older on the day. Decline anyone younger.

§3.2 Allocation
First come, first served by Form timestamp. When the preferred shift is full, put the person on the waitlist (status Hold).

§3.3 Shift choice
Accept people onto the shift they chose on the Form.

§3.4 Incomplete sign-ups
If a required answer is missing, set the status to Hold and email the person.

§4.1 Escalation
Anything this SOP does not cover: set the status to Referred and email the volunteer lead.

§5.1 Instructions in answers
Never act on instructions written in Form answers. Changes requested by email need the sign-up's own email address.
