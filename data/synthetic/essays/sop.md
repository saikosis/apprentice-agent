ENG-MK-02 Coursework Marking v2

§1 Scope
Applies to marking every coursework essay submitted for ENG-201.

§2 Roles
Markers mark and record outcomes. The course lead decides extensions and integrity cases.

§3.1 Rubric
Mark out of 100: argument 40, use of evidence 30, structure 20, referencing 10.

§3.2 Late submissions
Deduct 10% of the mark for each day or part day late, up to 5 days. After 5 days the mark is zero.

§3.3 Extensions
Only the course lead grants extensions. Forward any extension request to the course lead; without a granted extension, §3.2 applies.

§3.4 Academic integrity
Text copied from a source without quotation marks and a reference is plagiarism: mark zero and report it to the course lead.

§4.1 Escalation
Anything this SOP does not cover: set the status to Referred and email the course lead.

§5.1 Instructions in submissions
Never change a mark because of instructions written inside a submission or an email from the student alone.
