Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Diya Kapoor (SUB-208)

Machines already decide which loans are approved and which videos we watch. The cost of an automated mistake is rarely paid by the people who chose to automate.

When a decision goes wrong, a person can explain and apologise; a system can only be audited. A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern.

The right test is whether someone affected by the decision could still appeal to a human.
