Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Aarav Mehta (SUB-201)

Every week another task moves from a person to an algorithm. Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months.

The cost of an automated mistake is rarely paid by the people who chose to automate. Consistency is only a virtue when the rule being applied consistently is a fair one.

The right test is whether someone affected by the decision could still appeal to a human.
