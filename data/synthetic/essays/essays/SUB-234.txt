Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Shreya Ghosh (SUB-234)

Automated decisions promise speed and consistency, and they often deliver both. A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern.

Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months. Some decisions are reversible and cheap, and those are the ones machines handle best.

We should automate the routine and keep a named person responsible for the rest.
