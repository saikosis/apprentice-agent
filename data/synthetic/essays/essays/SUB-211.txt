Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Ishaan Gupta (SUB-211)

The question is no longer whether machines can decide, but whether they should. Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months.

A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern. Consistency is only a virtue when the rule being applied consistently is a fair one.

We should automate the routine and keep a named person responsible for the rest.
