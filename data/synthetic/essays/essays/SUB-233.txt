Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Nikhil Jain (SUB-233)

Automated decisions promise speed and consistency, and they often deliver both. When a decision goes wrong, a person can explain and apologise; a system can only be audited.

Some decisions are reversible and cheap, and those are the ones machines handle best. A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern.

Machines should advise, and people should decide when the stakes are high.
