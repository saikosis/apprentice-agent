Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Mehul Doshi (SUB-305)

Machines already decide which loans are approved and which videos we watch. A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern.

When a decision goes wrong, a person can explain and apologise; a system can only be audited. Consistency is only a virtue when the rule being applied consistently is a fair one.

We should automate the routine and keep a named person responsible for the rest.
