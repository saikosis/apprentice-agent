Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Arnav Bhalla (SUB-302)

Every week another task moves from a person to an algorithm. Some decisions are reversible and cheap, and those are the ones machines handle best.

Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months. Consistency is only a virtue when the rule being applied consistently is a fair one.

We should automate the routine and keep a named person responsible for the rest.
