Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Anjali Desai (SUB-232)

Machines already decide which loans are approved and which videos we watch. A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern.

Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months. When a decision goes wrong, a person can explain and apologise; a system can only be audited.

The right test is whether someone affected by the decision could still appeal to a human.
