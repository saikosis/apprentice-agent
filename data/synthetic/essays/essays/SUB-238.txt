Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Lakshmi Subramanian (SUB-238)

Every week another task moves from a person to an algorithm. A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern.

Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months. Some decisions are reversible and cheap, and those are the ones machines handle best.

The right test is whether someone affected by the decision could still appeal to a human.
