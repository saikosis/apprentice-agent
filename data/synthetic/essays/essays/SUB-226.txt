Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Zara Ali (SUB-226)

Machines already decide which loans are approved and which videos we watch. When a decision goes wrong, a person can explain and apologise; a system can only be audited.

Automation does not remove judgment from work; it moves judgment to the people who design the system and hides it from the people who live with its results. Consistency is only a virtue when the rule being applied consistently is a fair one. The cost of an automated mistake is rarely paid by the people who chose to automate.

Machines should advise, and people should decide when the stakes are high.
