Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Arjun Nair (SUB-207)

Machines already decide which loans are approved and which videos we watch. Some decisions are reversible and cheap, and those are the ones machines handle best.

Automation does not remove judgment from work; it moves judgment to the people who design the system and hides it from the people who live with its results. When a decision goes wrong, a person can explain and apologise; a system can only be audited. Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months.

The right test is whether someone affected by the decision could still appeal to a human.
