Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Aditya Joshi (SUB-213)

Machines already decide which loans are approved and which videos we watch. Consistency is only a virtue when the rule being applied consistently is a fair one.

A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern. The cost of an automated mistake is rarely paid by the people who chose to automate.

We should automate the routine and keep a named person responsible for the rest.
