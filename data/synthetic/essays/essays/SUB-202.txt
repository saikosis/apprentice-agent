Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Priya Ramesh (SUB-202)

Machines already decide which loans are approved and which videos we watch. Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months.

The cost of an automated mistake is rarely paid by the people who chose to automate. Consistency is only a virtue when the rule being applied consistently is a fair one.

We should automate the routine and keep a named person responsible for the rest.
