Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Riya Sen (SUB-220)

Every week another task moves from a person to an algorithm. Consistency is only a virtue when the rule being applied consistently is a fair one.

The cost of an automated mistake is rarely paid by the people who chose to automate. Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months.

We should automate the routine and keep a named person responsible for the rest.
