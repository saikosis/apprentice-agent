Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Rahul Bose (SUB-215)

Automated decisions promise speed and consistency, and they often deliver both. Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months.

Some decisions are reversible and cheap, and those are the ones machines handle best. A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern.

We should automate the routine and keep a named person responsible for the rest.
