Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Sneha Reddy (SUB-228)

Machines already decide which loans are approved and which videos we watch. When a decision goes wrong, a person can explain and apologise; a system can only be audited.

As Okafor puts it, "Automation does not remove judgment from work; it moves judgment to the people who design the system and hides it from the people who live with its results." (Okafor, 2024, p. 41). Some decisions are reversible and cheap, and those are the ones machines handle best. Consistency is only a virtue when the rule being applied consistently is a fair one.

We should automate the routine and keep a named person responsible for the rest.

References
Okafor, A. (2024). Machines and Meaning. Lagos: Harbour Press, p. 41.
