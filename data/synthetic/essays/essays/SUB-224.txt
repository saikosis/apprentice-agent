Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Pooja Hegde (SUB-224)

The question is no longer whether machines can decide, but whether they should. When a decision goes wrong, a person can explain and apologise; a system can only be audited.

Consistency is only a virtue when the rule being applied consistently is a fair one. Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months.

We should automate the routine and keep a named person responsible for the rest.
