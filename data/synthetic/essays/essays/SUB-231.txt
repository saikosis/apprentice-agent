Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Varun Kumar (SUB-231)

Every week another task moves from a person to an algorithm. A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern.

When a decision goes wrong, a person can explain and apologise; a system can only be audited. The cost of an automated mistake is rarely paid by the people who chose to automate.

Machines should advise, and people should decide when the stakes are high.
