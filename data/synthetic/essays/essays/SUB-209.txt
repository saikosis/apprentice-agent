Essay 2: Should we let machines decide?
Vikram Rao (SUB-209)

Machines already decide which loans are approved and which videos we watch. A hospital that triages patients by software can see more people, but it can also miss the patient who does not fit the pattern.

When a decision goes wrong, a person can explain and apologise; a system can only be audited. Workers who supervise automated systems report that they stop questioning the output after a few months.

Machines should advise, and people should decide when the stakes are high.
